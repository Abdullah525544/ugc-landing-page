09 — Questions

Frequently Asked.

A few common questions. If yours isn't here, ask on the call.

What is UGC advertising? +

UGC stands for user-generated content. In advertising, UGC-style ads are videos that feel natural and creator-led — even when produced by a brand or studio. They work because they look and feel like real content on the platform, which makes them easier to watch and more likely to convert.

Can you create videos for my specific product? +

Yes. Each project starts with understanding the product, audience, and offer. From there, I develop a creative angle built specifically around what makes your product worth stopping for.

What type of products do you work with? +

I work with physical product brands across beauty, skincare, haircare, fragrance, fashion, accessories, and lifestyle. If it can be filmed and benefits from short-form video, it's a fit.

Can these videos be used for Meta Ads? +

Yes. All videos are produced in vertical 9:16 and structured for paid placements including Meta Ads, Instagram Reels, TikTok, and other short-form platforms.

Do you create different hooks and concepts? +

Yes. I produce multiple hooks and angles so brands can test creatives against each other and identify what actually performs.

How long does a project take? +

Timing depends on the number of creatives and production requirements. We'll map the timeline together once the brief is clear.

How do I get started? +

Book a 30-minute creative call. We'll go through your product, audience, and goals, and I'll outline a creative direction you can move forward with.
